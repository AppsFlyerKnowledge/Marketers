| Section | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **What’s New** | Starting October 1, 2025:<br />- The **Retention Dashboard** will no longer be available.<br />  <br />- The **Activity Dashboard** will redirect to the new **Activity View** page in [My Dashboards.](/docs/my-dashboards)<br />  <br />If needed, you can still access the old Activity Dashboard by clicking **Go back to legacy activity**. |
| **Effective Date** | October 1, 2025 |
| **What you need to know** | As part of the transition from the Activity Dashboard to Activity View, AppsFlyer is introducing an updated flow to help ensure a smoother customer experience during the transition period.<br />When you click **Activity Dashboard** under the **Analyze** tab, you will be redirected to the **Activity View**. To access the legacy Activity Dashboard, click **Go back to legacy activity**.<br />Use [My Dashboards](/docs/my-dashboards) to recreate saved views or reports currently based on the Activity or Retention dashboards. [My Dashboards](/docs/my-dashboards) offers advanced customization and visualization options for improved insight.<br />This change is part of our continuous efforts to provide a more powerful and flexible analytics experience. [My Dashboards](/docs/my-dashboards) includes enhanced features such as:<br />- Customizable widget layouts<br />  <br />- Filterable and savable views<br />  <br />- Improved chart options |
| **What you need to do** | Transition to [My Dashboards](/docs/my-dashboards) to continue analyzing performance and retention trends. |
