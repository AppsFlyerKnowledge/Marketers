| **What's new** | Ad revenue calculations in Cohort reports will be fully aligned with LTV reports. **This only impacts customers with non-UTC timezone settings.** |
| **Effective date** | August 3, 2022 |
| **Where** | - Cohort dashboard<br />- Cohort API<br />- Cohort analytics via Data Locker |
| **What you must know** | - You don't need to do anything.<br />- Customers in non-UTC timezones should expect the following experiences:<br />  - **UTC+** timezones should expect a drop in ad revenue on Day 0 and a spike on Day 1 starting on the effective date.<br />  - **UTC-** timezones should expect a spike on Day 0 and a drop on Day 1 starting on the effective date. |
