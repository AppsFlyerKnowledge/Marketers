| **What's new** | Ad revenue calculations in Cohort reports will be fully aligned with LTV reports. **This only impacts customers with non-UTC timezone settings.** |
| --- | --- |
| **Effective date** | August 3, 2022 |
| **Where** | <ul><li>Cohort dashboard</li><li>Cohort API</li><li>Cohort analytics via Data Locker</li></ul> |
| **What you must know** | <ul><li>You don't need to do anything.</li><li>Customers in non-UTC timezones should expect the following experiences:<ul><li>**UTC+** timezones should expect a drop in ad revenue on Day 0 and a spike on Day 1 starting on the effective date.</li><li>**UTC-** timezones should expect a spike on Day 0 and a drop on Day 1 starting on the effective date.</li></ul></li></ul> |
