| **What's changing** | Attribute ad revenue generated during the SKAdNetwork activity window to the campaign bringing the user. Currently, this ability is limited to advertisers integrated with generic impression-level ad revenue via SDK. |
| --- | --- |
| **Bulletin date** | August 16, 2021 |
| **Action required** | To record ad revenue in your SKAdNetwork dashboard:<ul><li>[Integrate the generic impression-level ad revenue SDK into your app.](/docs/integrate-the-roi360-ad-revenue-sdk-api)</li></ul> |
| **Monetization networks mediated by MoPub** | AdColony, AdMob (Google), Google Ad Manager (Google), AppLovin, Chartboost, Meta Audience Network, Fyber, InMobi, ironSource, Mintegral, Ogury, Pangle, Snap Audience Network, Tapjoy, Unity Ads, Yahoo, Vungle |
