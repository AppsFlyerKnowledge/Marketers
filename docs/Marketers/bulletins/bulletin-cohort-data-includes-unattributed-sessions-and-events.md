| **What's new** | [Unattributed sessions and events](/docs/measure-reinstalls#inapp-events-and-how-they-are-attributed) appear in the Cohort dashboard and reports. |
| **Effective date** | May 26, 2024 |
| **Where** | - Cohort and Retention dashboard<br />- Cohort API<br />- Cohort reports via Data Locker<br />- Advanced aggregated reports via Data Locker [Closed beta] |
| **What you must know** | - Until the effective date, [unattributed sessions and events](/docs/measure-reinstalls#inapp-events-and-how-they-are-attributed) weren't included in the Cohort dashboard and reports. This sometimes caused discrepancies between Cohort other reporting methods, where Cohort showed fewer organic sessions and events (and revenue).<br />- From the effective date, unattributed sessions and events appear in the Cohort dashboard and reports with "organic" media source. This means:<br />  - You may see more organic data in Cohort data than before.<br />  - There won't be any more discrepancies between Cohort and other reporting methods caused by unattributed sessions and events. |
