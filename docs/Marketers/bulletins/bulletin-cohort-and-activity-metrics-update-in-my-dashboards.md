| **What’s new?** | Starting **July 2026**, Cohort and Activity metrics in **My Dashboards** are moving to upgraded data infrastructure designed to support **AI-ready data**. These changes will roll out gradually to clients throughout **July - August 2026**.<br /><br />As part of this gradual rollout, you may notice changes to some metric values, historical data availability, and segmentation options.<br /><br />This update introduces new view types and metrics for Activity data, improves Unique User accuracy in Cohort data, and expands historical data and segmentation options across both Cohort and Activity. |
| --- | --- |
| **Effective date** | July - August 2026 gradual rollout. |
| **Who needs to do it** | All advertisers, agencies, and partners using Cohort or Activity metrics in **My Dashboards**. |
| **Required actions** | No action is required. The changes apply automatically. |
| **What you should know** | ### Retargeting and UA view types now available for Activity<br /><br />Activity data can now be filtered and analyzed by view type, including Retargeting and UA.<br /><br />* Retargeting data is available from February 2025.<br />* UA data is available from February 2026.<br />* Same-day data is not included for Retargeting and UA views. Data lags by 1 day.<br /><br />### Net Revenue metrics now available for Cohort and Activity<br /><br />Clients measuring net revenue via **ROI360 Store revenue** or by passing the `af_net_revenue` event parameter in their in-app events will now see:<br /><br />* Net Revenue<br />* Net ROAS<br />* Net ROI<br />* Net ARPU<br />* Net Profit<br /><br />These metrics are only visible to clients sending net revenue data.<br /><br />### CTV app support now available for Cohort and Activity<br /><br />Cohort and Activity metrics are now available for CTV apps.<br /><br />### New dimensions now available for Cohort and Activity<br /><br />The following dimensions are now available for segmentation in both Cohort and Activity:<br /><br />* Platform<br />* Sub 1<br />* Engagement destination<br /><br />### Extended historical data for Cohort<br /><br />For most Cohort metrics, you now have access to data from **May 6, 2024** onward, adding about 6 more months of history than before.<br /><br />This does not apply to Unique Users daily data, which starts from **June 3, 2025**.<br /><br />### Unique Users updated calculation method for Cohort daily breakdowns<br /><br />The following metrics now use an updated calculation method for daily Cohort breakdowns, such as Day 1, Day 7, and other daily breakdowns:<br /><br />* In-app event Unique Users<br />* Retention<br />* Retention Rate<br />* ARPU<br /><br />#### What changed and why<br /><br />Users are now identified at the device level. A user who re-engaged multiple times is counted once, not once per re-engagement.<br /><br />This corrects previous overcounting for apps running re-engagement campaigns.<br /><br />If your app runs re-engagement campaigns, you may see daily Unique User numbers decrease. This is a correction, not a drop in actual users or performance.<br /><br />The new method uses a probabilistic counting approach, which introduces a very small margin of approximation, typically within 1–2%. This is expected behavior, not a data error.<br /><br />#### Data availability<br /><br />* Daily granularity data is available from **June 3, 2025** onward.<br />* Earlier daily Unique Users data is not available in My Dashboards.<br />* Weekly and monthly granularity are not affected. Full history remains available.<br /><br />### Unique Users and Active Users historical availability for Activity<br /><br />For dates before **June 3, 2025**, Unique Users and Active Users in Activity are only available broken down by date.<br /><br />Totals across a date range are available from **June 3, 2025** onward.<br /><br />This applies to any metric calculated from Unique Users or Active Users. This is a permanent limitation.<br /><br />### Uninstalls temporarily unavailable for Cohort<br /><br />The Uninstalls metric is temporarily unavailable.<br /><br />**LTV is not affected.**<br /><br />### Ad revenue unified in UTC across all dashboards<br /><br />Ad revenue metrics across all dashboards now display in UTC, regardless of integration type or app-level timezone settings. Historical data isn't affected.<br /><br />* This change applies to ad revenue collected from `impression-level (SDK)` integration type. Data from other integration types was already reported in UTC.<br />* We made this change to normalize ad revenue collected from multiple sources and partners, most of which report their data in UTC.<br />* On the change date (July 20th), if your app uses a non-UTC time zone in your App settings, you may notice a lift in your ad revenue figures. This isn't data duplication or new data, and nothing is deleted. It's the same data shifted to a different day to align with UTC.<br /><br />## About the transition<br /><br />The goal of this migration is to move Cohort and Activity data to a more modern and scalable infrastructure that improves data reliability and performance, enables AI-ready analytics capabilities, and supports future product improvements across AppsFlyer’s aggregated attribution data.<br /><br />If you need to reference older data, the Cohort & Retention dashboard remains available. Use it to access Cohort Unique Users data from before **June 3, 2025**, or to view Uninstalls data while the metric is being rebuilt. |
