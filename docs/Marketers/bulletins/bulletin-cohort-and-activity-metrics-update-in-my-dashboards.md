| **What’s new?** | Starting **July 2026**, Cohort and Activity metrics in **My Dashboards** are moving to upgraded data infrastructure designed to support **AI-ready data**. These changes will roll out gradually to clients throughout **July - August 2026**.<br /><br />As part of this gradual rollout, you may notice changes to some metric values, historical data availability, and segmentation options.<br /><br />This update introduces new view types and metrics for Activity data, improves Unique User accuracy in Cohort data, and expands historical data and segmentation options across both Cohort and Activity. |
| --- | --- |
| **Effective date** | July - August 2026 gradual rollout. |
| **Who needs to do it** | All advertisers, agencies, and partners using Cohort or Activity metrics in **My Dashboards**. |
| **Required actions** | No action is required. The changes apply automatically. |
| **What you should know** | **<span id="h_activity-retargeting-and-ua-view-types"></span>Retargeting and UA view types now available for Activity**<br /><br />Activity data can now be filtered and analyzed by view type, including Retargeting and UA.<ul><li>Retargeting data is available from February 2025.</li><li>UA data is available from February 2026.</li><li>Same-day data is not included for Retargeting and UA views. Data lags by 1 day.</li></ul>**<span id="h_net-revenue-metrics"></span>Net Revenue metrics now available for Cohort and Activity**<br /><br />Clients measuring net revenue via **ROI360 Store revenue** or by passing the `af_net_revenue` event parameter in their in-app events will now see:<ul><li>Net Revenue</li><li>Net ROAS</li><li>Net ROI</li><li>Net ARPU</li><li>Net Profit</li></ul>These metrics are only visible to clients sending net revenue data.<br /><br />**<span id="h_ctv-app-support"></span>CTV app support now available for Cohort and Activity**<br /><br />Cohort and Activity metrics are now available for CTV apps.<br /><br />**<span id="h_new-dimensions"></span>New dimensions now available for Cohort and Activity**<br /><br />The following dimensions are now available for segmentation in both Cohort and Activity:<ul><li>Platform</li><li>Sub 1</li><li>Engagement destination</li></ul>**<span id="h_extended-historical-data"></span>Extended historical data for Cohort**<br /><br />For most Cohort metrics, you now have access to data from **May 6, 2024** onward, adding about 6 more months of history than before.<br /><br />This does not apply to Unique Users daily data, which starts from **June 3, 2025**.<br /><br />**<span id="h_unique-users-updated-calculation-method"></span>Unique Users updated calculation method for Cohort daily breakdowns**<br /><br />The following metrics now use an updated calculation method for daily Cohort breakdowns, such as Day 1, Day 7, and other daily breakdowns:<ul><li>In-app event Unique Users</li><li>Retention</li><li>Retention Rate</li><li>ARPU</li></ul>**<span id="h_what-changed-and-why"></span>What changed and why**<br /><br />Users are now identified at the device level. A user who re-engaged multiple times is counted once, not once per re-engagement.<br /><br />This corrects previous overcounting for apps running re-engagement campaigns.<br /><br />If your app runs re-engagement campaigns, you may see daily Unique User numbers decrease. This is a correction, not a drop in actual users or performance.<br /><br />The new method uses a probabilistic counting approach, which introduces a very small margin of approximation, typically within 1–2%. This is expected behavior, not a data error.<br /><br />**<span id="h_unique-users-data-availability"></span>Data availability**<ul><li>Daily granularity data is available from **June 3, 2025** onward.</li><li>Earlier daily Unique Users data is not available in My Dashboards.</li><li>Weekly and monthly granularity are not affected. Full history remains available.</li></ul>**<span id="h_activity-unique-users-and-active-users-historical-availability"></span>Unique Users and Active Users historical availability for Activity**<br /><br />For dates before **June 3, 2025**, Unique Users and Active Users in Activity are only available broken down by date.<br /><br />Totals across a date range are available from **June 3, 2025** onward.<br /><br />This applies to any metric calculated from Unique Users or Active Users. This is a permanent limitation.<br /><br />**<span id="h_uninstalls-temporarily-unavailable"></span>Uninstalls temporarily unavailable for Cohort**<br /><br />The Uninstalls metric is temporarily unavailable.<br /><br />**LTV is not affected.**<br /><br />**<span id="h_ad-revenue-unified-in-utc"></span>Ad revenue unified in UTC across all dashboards**<br /><br />Ad revenue metrics across all dashboards now display in UTC, regardless of integration type or app-level timezone settings. Historical data isn't affected.<ul><li>This change applies to ad revenue collected from `impression-level (SDK)` integration type. Data from other integration types was already reported in UTC.</li><li>We made this change to normalize ad revenue collected from multiple sources and partners, most of which report their data in UTC.</li><li>On the change date (July 20th), if your app uses a non-UTC time zone in your App settings, you may notice a lift in your ad revenue figures. This isn't data duplication or new data, and nothing is deleted. It's the same data shifted to a different day to align with UTC.</li></ul>**<span id="h_about-the-transition"></span>About the transition**<br /><br />The goal of this migration is to move Cohort and Activity data to a more modern and scalable infrastructure that improves data reliability and performance, enables AI-ready analytics capabilities, and supports future product improvements across AppsFlyer’s aggregated attribution data.<br /><br />If you need to reference older data, the Cohort & Retention dashboard remains available. Use it to access Cohort Unique Users data from before **June 3, 2025**, or to view Uninstalls data while the metric is being rebuilt. |
