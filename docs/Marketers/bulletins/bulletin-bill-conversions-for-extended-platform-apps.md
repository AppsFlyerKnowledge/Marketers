| **What's new** | Starting February 1, 2026, non-organic installs from extended platform apps (for example, CTV, PC, and console) are included in billable conversions, according to your existing conversion-based commercial terms. |
| **Effective date** | February 1, 2026 |
| **Who's affected** | Customers with extended platform apps on conversion packages or Adaptive Pricing. |
| **Recommended action** | Review expected conversion volume and Adaptive Pricing credit usage. |
| **Additional information** | Billing applies to conversions generated by extended platform apps. This includes apps running on CTV, PC, console, and other non-mobile platforms that aren’t iOS, Android, Windows phone, PBA, or website.<br />#### **What you should know**<br />- Only **non-organic installs** are billed<br />- **Retargeting isn’t supported** for extended platforms<br />- **Owned media activity** is included<br />- Conversion unit cost is **the same as mobile conversions**<br />- No additional fees apply at launch<br />#### **Adaptive Pricing considerations**<br />If you use Adaptive Pricing, billing extended platform conversions can increase credit consumption related to:<br />- Non-organic installs<br />- ROI360 cost and revenue measurement<br />- Pull API rows<br />- Data Locker raw and aggregated reports<br />**What’s not included**<br />- MAU-based billing isn’t currently supported for extended platform apps |
