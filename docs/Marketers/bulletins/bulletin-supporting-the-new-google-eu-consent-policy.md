| <span id="whats-new"></span>**What's new** | As part of Google’s preparations for the enforcement of the <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/priorities-2019-2024/europe-fit-digital-age/digital-markets-act-ensuring-fair-and-open-digital-markets_en">Digital Markets Act</Anchor> (DMA), Google is updating their <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://www.google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy/?sjid=15416941068016224808-NA">EU user consent policy</Anchor>. AppsFlyer is making the necessary changes to support Google’s policy requirements while ensuring that advertisers maximize the value of their Google Ads marketing channels. |
| --- | --- |
| <span id="effective-date"></span>**Effective date** | March 6, 2024 |
| <span id="relevant-integrations-and-audience"></span>**Relevant integrations and audience** | All advertisers with apps integrated with Google Ads or GMP DV360 (googleadwords\_int, dv360\_int), that have end users based in the <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Glossary:European_Economic_Area_(EEA)">European Economic Area (EEA)</Anchor>. |
| <span id="required-actions"></span>**Required actions** | To comply with Google's requirements, advertisers must collect consent from EEA users for personal data usage and send them to Google. AppsFlyer facilitates this process by sending the consent signals to Google using the AppsFlyer SDK. Update your AppsFlyer SDK to ensure compliance. <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/support-google-eu-consent-policy">See all the details here</Anchor>. |
