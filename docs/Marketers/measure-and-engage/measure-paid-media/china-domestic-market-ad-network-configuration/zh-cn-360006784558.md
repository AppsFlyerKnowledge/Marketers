【概要】：本文讲解了如何针对爱奇艺麒麟启用AppsFlyer的归因与衡量功能，以便您在AppsFlyer面板上查看爱奇艺麒麟广告的归因数据。

请根据以下步骤来配置爱奇艺广告平台应用推广广告的追踪。

## AppsFlyer配置

1. 进入渠道配置页面(“Integrated Partners”)

   进入您想要推广的产品相应页面, 从左边导航栏点击”Integrated Partners”进入渠道追踪配置页面。

   ![iqiyi-appsflyer-1_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140357/iqiyi-appsflyer-1_zh-cn.png)
2. 搜索选择针对爱奇艺广告（IQIYI)的追踪配置页面

   进入 Integrated Partners 页面后, 在相应搜索框输入iqiyi,进一步点击搜索按钮。

   ![iqiyi-appsflyer-2_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140437/iqiyi-appsflyer-2_zh-cn.png)
3. 点击iQIYI打开广告追踪配置窗口

   进入iQIYI的配置页面，在第一个默认的选项卡”渠道对接”内

   1. 打开激活开关，即可开启iQIYI归因

      ![iqiyi-appsflyer-3_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140457/iqiyi-appsflyer-3_zh-cn.png)
   2. 在第二个选项卡”归因链接”内进行相关配置并点击如图所示按钮复制生成的追踪链接

      ![iqiyi-appsflyer-4_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007217198/iqiyi-appsflyer-4_zh-cn.png)

## 爱奇艺奇麟神算广告平台配置

1. 登录爱奇艺奇麟神算广告平台（[https://tuiguang.iqiyi.com](about:blank))
   1. 选择工具箱>转化追踪工具>新建转化>应用下载API，如图所示：

      ![iqiyi-appsflyer-5_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140517/iqiyi-appsflyer-5_zh-cn.png)

      ![iqiyi-appsflyer-6_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140537/iqiyi-appsflyer-6_zh-cn.png)
   2. 填写信息，并将从AppsFlyer后台生成的追踪链接填入点击监测地址，提交，根据 提示进行联调

      ![iqiyi-appsflyer-7_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007217278/iqiyi-appsflyer-7_zh-cn.png)

      ![iqiyi-appsflyer-8_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140557/iqiyi-appsflyer-8_zh-cn.png)

      ![iqiyi-appsflyer-9_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140577/iqiyi-appsflyer-9_zh-cn.png)
2. 联调成功后，回到奇麟首页，选择广告管理>广告系列>广告>创意，根据需求填写信息

   ![iqiyi-appsflyer-10_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140597/iqiyi-appsflyer-10_zh-cn.png)

   这里选择转化目标，如未出现刚才新建的转化目标，请登出账户并重新登录

   ![iqiyi-appsflyer-11_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140637/iqiyi-appsflyer-11_zh-cn.png)

   将追踪链接填入如图所示，开启广告即可

   ![iqiyi-appsflyer-12_zh-cn.png](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/article_attachments/360007140657/iqiyi-appsflyer-12_zh-cn.png)
