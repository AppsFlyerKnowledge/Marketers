title: "Google AdMob—Ad revenue attribution configuration"
excerpt: "Configure Google AdMob with AppsFlyer to receive aggregate and impression-level ad revenue data for attribution."
---
Google AdMob, an AppsFlyer integrated partner, allows app owners to receive ad revenue data to be used for ad revenue attribution.

AdMob provides [ad revenue attribution](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/217490046) at the aggregate and impression levels. See also: [Generic impression-level ad revenue SDK connector integration guide](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/4416353506833)

> 📘 Prerequisite
>
> Before you set up the integration, make sure you [contact the partner](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/9960361873809#contact-details) and open an account with them.

**To enable the ad revenue integration,** follow [these integration instructions](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/217490046-Ad-revenue-attribution-guide#connect-to-ad-revenue-integrated-partners).
