## Use Partner Integrations

<Accordion title="What information can I see on a partner card without entering the page?">

To make it easier for you to evaluate the partner without going to the partner page for more details, you can see the following information on [partner cards](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/9960361873809--Beta-Marketplace-Overview-#partner-cards):

- Quality badges (for partners who meet quality criteria): [**Performance Index**](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/performance-index/) and **High-quality traffic**—indicating a low-fraud rate
- The partner’s top three supported capabilities
- Type of media platform
- Integration status (when [Active](/docs/partner-activation-deactivation))

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What filters are available for searching partners?">

AppsFlyer Partner Integrations allow you to use filters to refine your search results. Use filters such as Partner type, Target region and geo, High-Quality traffic, and AppsFlyer performance index. See the [description of each filter.](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/9960361873809-Partner-Marketplace?query=marketplace#filters)

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I integrate with new partners?">

The integration itself is done on the partner integration setup page, but to find new partners and connect with them, you must go through Partner Integrations. The Marketplace tab enables you to browse and search for partners available for integration with the selected app, review their capabilities, and start the integration process.

Learn [how to integrate with new partners in Partner Integrations](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/9960361873809--WIP-Beta-Marketplace-Overview-?query=marketplace#integrate-with-a-new-partner)

</Accordion>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="If I see an interesting partner without any contact details—what should I do?">

We’re doing our best to make sure all partners provide their contact details, but in case it’s still missing, reach out to your contact person in AppsFlyer.

</Accordion>
