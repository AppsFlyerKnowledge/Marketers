Adquant, an AppsFlyer integrated partner (formerly known as Facebook Marketing Partners), is a leading provider of Artificial Intelligence solutions to help app marketers scale their growth.

## Setting up Adquant

> 📘 Prerequisite
>
> An account with the partner. If you don't have an account, contact the partner to open one before setting up the integration.

**New integration:**

1. In AppsFlyer, go to **Collaborate > Partner Integrations > Marketplace** tab.
2. Select the relevant app.
3. Search for Adquant and select **Integrate**.
4. In the **Integration** tab, turn on the **Activate partner** toggle. Keep it on as long as you work with Adquant.
5. Configure remaining tabs as needed.

**Edit existing integration:**

In AppsFlyer, go to **Collaborate > Partner Integrations > Active integrations** tab, search for the partner, and make your changes.

Learn more about [partner integrations](/docs/set-up-an-integrated-partner).

### In-app events

1. To send in-app event postbacks to Bidalgo, turn on **In-app event postbacks**.
2. Set the configuration according to the guidelines [here](/docs/meta-ads-in-app-event-mapping#predefined-event-mapping).
3. Click **Save**.
