## About the Creative Optimization Discovery AI dashboard

The [**Creative Optimization**](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/18564718580625) dashboards let you measure the performance of your creatives by pulling all your creatives from all media channels into one place. Analyze their performance by selected dimensions, across multiple campaigns, channels, and more, to gain critical insights before planning, selecting, and producing your next batch of creatives.

The AI-powered **Creative Optimization** **Discovery AI dashboard** gives your creative team all the info they need to produce winning creatives. It provides granular performance data, enabling informed decisions based on the specific elements or features within a creative that contribute to its effectiveness. With this dashboard, you can consistently produce and maintain successful creatives that are data-driven and optimized for maximum impact. Based on the main filter selections, you can view a gallery of your creatives, or break them down into individual scenes and elements.

## Procedures—Using the Discovery AI dashboard

### Set up the dashboard

**To set up the Discovery AI dashboard to view data about specific creatives**:

1. In the AppsFlyer side menu, go to **Optimize** \> **Creative Optimization**, and click the **Discovery AI** tab.
2. In the top-level filter bar, select the apps, date range, and other filters for which you want to get data about. [Learn more](/docs/creative-optimization-guide#filter-bar)
3. Use the search bar or the preset [quick queries](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/18565941422993-Creative-Optimization-Discovery-dashboard#quick-queries), to find specific creatives, or specific elements, texts, etc. that appear within creatives.
4. [Coming soon] Use the Discovery AI search to find specific creatives, or specific elements, texts, etc. that appear within creatives. [Learn more about Discovery AI search](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/18565941422993-Creative-Optimization-Discovery-dashboard#discovery-ai-search).
   1. Write a search term/element description in the search bar and click **Enter**.
   2. [Optional] Click **Save as Smart tag** to add your search term to the smart tag list for future use. See the [Smart tags section](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/18565941422993-Creative-Optimization-Discovery-dashboard#[coming-soon]-smart-tags) for more information.
5. [Coming soon] If you already have Smart tags saved, use them to find specific creatives, or specific elements, texts, etc. that appear within creatives. [Learn more about Smart tags](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/18565941422993-Creative-Optimization-Discovery-dashboard#[coming-soon]-smart-tags).
   1. Click the specific tag/query you want (multiple queries can be selected) and the gallery will be filtered according to your selections.
   2. [Optional] Click **Compare** to compare the group performance of up to 4 Smart tags/queries side by side.
6. View the discovery card gallery of all the creatives, elements, scenes, or texts that match your filters and search settings.
   1. [Optional] To change the metrics displayed on the cards, click the **Settings** icon, and select up to 5 metrics to display. [Learn more about discovery card settings](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/18565941422993-Creative-Optimization-Discovery-dashboard#discovery-tabs).
   2. Sort the gallery tiles from highest to lowest (or the reverse) by the metrics. Metrics available to sort by are those selected in the discovery card settings.

      ![Sort by new.jpg](https://files.readme.io/8a8d5a0f13226bc854ae54f389f7a1e5a838f0bcf8b9af758a6a7e9efceb502e-24289989225105.jpg)
7. Click on a specific card to drill down deeper and get additional performance data and insights.

## Concepts

### AI search

The AI-powered search bar lets you analyze how specific creative elements impact performance, from a single word to full visual concepts and storylines.

Use it to isolate patterns, validate hypotheses, and identify which elements drive stronger results.

**To use search:**

1. Use the filters at the top to define the creatives you want to analyze, for example, by app or date range.

> 📘 Filter Priority
>
> The top-level Creative Optimization [filter](/docs/creative-optimization-guide#filter-bar) is the primary one. Search filters those results further. This means that the top-level filter will override search in case of conflicting requests.

2. Enter a natural language query in the Discovery AI search bar describing what you want to explore.

Search queries should refer only to creative content, such as visuals, text, audio, themes, or story elements. Do not search by performance metrics, for example, "creatives with IPM above 2," or by creative name.

#### What you can search for

Search supports natural language queries across multiple creative content dimensions. You can combine elements to generate more precise and contextual results.

- ##### **Visual**

Search for visual concepts such as:
  - Character appearance, actions, and object interactions
  - Backgrounds, environments, dominant colors, and settings
  - Timing within the creative

**Examples:**

“Creatives with a beach at sunset in the first 5 seconds”

“Urban street scene with race cars”

- ##### **Text**

Search for specific messaging, headlines, or microcopy.

**Examples:**

“Creatives showing a value proposition about saving time”

“Download now” call to action

“Spanish subtitles”

- ##### **Audio**

Search for audio elements such as voiceovers, accents or languages, music style or tempo, and sound effects.

**Examples:**

“Fast-tempo background music”

“Motivational voiceover”

- ##### **Narrative and high-level concepts**

Search for broader themes, storylines, and emotional tones.

**Examples:**

“Happy storyline”

“Dramatic gameplay scene”

“Problem-solution narrative”

#### Combining properties

Combine multiple elements in a single query for more precise analysis.

Examples:

“Female character in a gym with motivational voiceover in the first 5 seconds”

“Puzzle gameplay with dramatic music and red background”

“Beach scene at sunset with happy storyline and soft piano music”

Combining attributes helps isolate clearer creative patterns and reduces noise in your analysis.

#### Notes

- Search supports natural language queries about creative content only.
- Search queries do not support:
  - Performance metrics, such as IPM, CTR, or retention
  - Creative names or IDs

### Smart tags

Smart tags are saved searches that you can refer back to whenever you need. Perhaps even more importantly, they tag all creatives with the name and search term, so you can use these Smart tags as group by dimensions for your creatives in the Overview dashboard. For example, if you create Smart tags called "Gameplay scenes" and "Animated scenes" in the Creative Optimization Overview dashboard you can group data by Smart Tags and get separate data rows for Gameplay scenes and Animated scenes.

> 📘 Filter Priority
>
> The top-level Creative Optimization [filter](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/18564718580625#creative-dashboard) is the primary one. Smart tags filter those results further. This means that the top-level filter will override Smart tags in case of conflicting requests.

**Example common queries you can use to create Smart tags:**

- Racing car in the first 5 seconds
- Creatives that contain multiple characters
- Creatives that contain compliance messaging
- Creatives that include app UI
- Creatives that contain actual gameplay in the first 5 seconds
- And more

### Quick queries

There are preset quick queries that you can use for searching creatives, scenes, elements, and texts. The presets are based on commonly-searched terms, and include:

- New creatives/scenes/elements (appearing for under 14 days)
- Long-running creatives/scenes/elements (appearing for over 180 days)
- Under 30 days
- Long videos (over 15 seconds)
- Short videos (under 15 seconds)
- Active creatives/scenes/elements (significant media spend)
- Animated scenes/elements
- Live scenes/elements
- Mixed scenes (both live and animated features)
- Gameplay scenes
- UGC scenes/elements (content that is created and shared by users)

> 📘 UGC Classification
>
> A UGC is determined on the creative level, so if an animated scene is part of a UGC, that scene (and the elements in the creative as well) will be considered a UGC.

- Shown on [SRNs](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/360001546905)
- Shown on [non-SRNs](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/212188826)
- Opening scenes
- End cards
- And more

### Discovery card drilldown

Discovery card drilldown lets you probe deeper and find:

- The same or similar creatives.
- The same or similar appearances of elements, scenes, and texts in other creatives.

For scenes and texts, you can click **Investigate** on a specific discovery card. This opens the **Appearances** tab, where you can:

- Get a list of all the creatives with the specific scene or text in a separate row. The performance metrics in each row are the same as those selected for the discovery cards.
- See which creatives have this specific scene or text.
- See and compare the performance of the creatives that contain the specific scene or text. For example, whether one creative had a higher volume of ad spend then another.
- Play the creatives.
- See the volume (time and percentage) of how much time the creative includes the scene or text. The parts of the creative that include the scene or text are marked in orange.
- See and compare where specific scenes or texts appear in creatives and use this information to connect the dots and correlate the scene or text in the creatives to performance metrics. For example, maybe the conversion rate is better when the “buy now” call-to-action appears earlier in a creative asset rather than in the beginning.

You can also switch to the **Similarities** tab, where you can get a list of all the creatives that are similar, or that have similar elements. The performance metrics in each row are the same as those selected for the discovery cards.

For creatives and elements, you can open the **Similarities** tab by clicking **Find similar** on a specific discovery card.
