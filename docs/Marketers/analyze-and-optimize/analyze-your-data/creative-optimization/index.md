    Follow the onboarding instructions in this article to get Creative Optimization up and running.

  <Card title="Creative Optimization guide" href="/docs/creative-optimization-guide" icon="fa-solid fa-palette">
    Creative Optimization enables you to pull all creative asset data from all your campaigns into a single platform, so you can analyze their performance, discover which creatives work best, and make better data-driven decisions. Data is LTV, meaning the metrics are for conversions that occurred from creatives during the selected date range.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creative Optimization Overview dashboard" href="/docs/creative-optimization-overview-dashboard" icon="fa-solid fa-palette">
    Use the Creative Optimization Overview dashboard to analyze creative performance, so you can choose the most effective creatives and maximize the impact of your campaigns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creative Optimization Discovery AI dashboard" href="/docs/creative-optimization-discovery-ai-dashboard" icon="fa-solid fa-robot">
    Use the Creative Optimization Discovery AI dashboard to analyze the performance of your creatives and the scenes and elements they're built from, to produce and maintain data-driven, winning creatives.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creative Optimization partner integrations" href="/docs/creative-optimization-partner-integrations" icon="fa-solid fa-palette">
    Set up partner integrations to automatically import all your creative data into Creative Optimization.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creative Management" href="/docs/creative-management" icon="fa-solid fa-palette">
    Creative Management helps you centralize, review, and launch creatives faster. Use it to sync creatives from your creative storage, understand creative activity status across media sources, easily find relevant creatives, and upload them directly to supported media source campaigns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creative Optimization ETL reporting" href="/docs/creative-optimization-etl-reporting" icon="fa-solid fa-palette">
    Creative Optimization ETL streams your creative performance data to cloud storage daily for direct loading into your business intelligence (BI) systems, with no manual exports needed. Choose between Standard Creative ETL , which delivers a fixed report structure, and Custom Creative ETL , which lets you choose exactly which dimensions, metrics, and events to include.
  </Card>
