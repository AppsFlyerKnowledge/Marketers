> **At a glance:** Collaborations enable commerce media networks and brands to leverage combined data to create new ad revenue streams and achieve maximum campaign results.

## About collaborations

The AppsFlyer <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-guide">Data Collaboration Platform (DCP)</Anchor> empowers commerce media networks and brands to partner up and unlock the potential of their first-party data to reach high-intent users. The secure, privacy-centric platform enables seamless collaborations, allowing partners to generate new ad revenue streams and build custom audiences from collaborative data for activation in the commerce media network or in media channels such as Meta, Google, and more.

## Create a collaboration

This section describes how to create a new collaboration.

**To create a collaboration, follow the steps below:**

<div id="step-1-access-collaborations" />

<Accordion title="Step 1. Access collaborations">

**To access DCP collaborations:**

1. Access the Data Collaboration Platform by clicking **Collaborate** > **Data Clean Room**, in the AppsFlyer side menu. 

   <Image alt="Click Data Clean Room.png" src="https://files.readme.io/3ed65908a57c2a9642bb3afc246abfa490d2039fa1219d1484ce30c9a0fa0f10-42204335550481.png" width="33.49%" />
2. (Optional) Add collaboration [Terms and Conditions](#terms-and-conditions). This can also be done later.
3. Go to the **Collaborations** tab > **+ New collaboration**.

   <Image alt="From collaborations tab click New collaboration w arrow New UI.png" src="https://files.readme.io/63945589565b79f71d82453c00df9e088fc9a9eaef6dd6a14ecc1130b96c19ca-43774064785425.png" width="1240px" />

   The new collaboration screen appears.

   [![New collaboration - empty state.png](https://files.readme.io/8f209f87aaf32315fed0fae46bd55e7bb101084ae6983b08e1566c64a89f0a8e-32192739280273.png)](https://files.readme.io/8f209f87aaf32315fed0fae46bd55e7bb101084ae6983b08e1566c64a89f0a8e-32192739280273.png)

</Accordion>

<div id="step-2-set-collaboration-details" />

<Accordion title="Step 2. Set collaboration details">

**To set the collaboration details:**

1. Click **Set** (under **Set collaboration details**).

   <Image alt="Set .png" src="https://files.readme.io/e47781f360714e539ed54c6a8bfaa32b282f4609dc2c91d77021034f46dfb23a-32348709077393.png" width="951px" />
2. Enter a collaboration name. This will be the initial name your collaborator will see as well (you can always change it later). Then, click **Save**.

<Image alt="Set collaboration details.png" src="https://files.readme.io/1af9e94bfac35aa85a8857e8aa117939245cf73f7c83700d2fe1c77bf12d6cc3-32324652610961.png" width="825px" />

</Accordion>

<div id="step-3-add-a-collaborator-optional" />

<Accordion title="Step 3. Add a collaborator (optional)">

A collaborator is the brand partner you invite to your collaboration. Once the collaborator accepts your invite, they will be able to see and use the sources you’ve associated with the collaboration, as specified in your data permissions policy. **This step is optional and can be done at any time** (during or after creation of the collaboration).

**To add a collaborator:**

1. Click **Add** (under **Add a collaborator**).   

   <Image alt="Add a collaborator click Add.png" src="https://files.readme.io/958f88782e8cd36bbfa1962b51de72824200952be183a968d311fa6ef164553e-34014450760465.png" width="2088px" />
2. Enter the collaborator's email.

   <Image alt="Set collaboration policy.png" src="https://files.readme.io/6e5f0f0780cbaa661bcfc711fe2deb53e199db813b78581563d1af566462f33b-41966029775633.png" width="86.66%" />
3. Click **Set** **collaboration policy** to define the permissions you grant to your collaborator, which specify what the collaborator can do with your data and which resources or activation partners they can use. In addition to this default policy, custom data permissions can also be [set per source](/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-create-a-collaboration#optional-set-custom-data-permissions-by-source). Learn more about <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-sharing-permissions">sharing permissions</Anchor>.
4. After saving the relevant permissions, click **Save** again.   
   Once the collaboration is created, the collaborator will receive a collaboration invite in their inbox. When they sign into AppsFlyer from the email, they'll be able to start collaborating with you.

</Accordion>

<div id="step-4-associate-sources-optional" />

<Accordion title="Step 4. Associate sources (optional)">

Associating sources means sharing your data (which includes <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-create-and-manage-sources">sources</Anchor> and/or <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-create-customized-data-views">data views</Anchor>) in the collaboration. This will give your collaborator access to view and use your sources as defined by you in your data permissions policy or by custom permissions defined per source. **This step is optional and can be done at any time** (during or after creation of the collaboration).

**To associate sources with the collaboration:**

1. In the **Data Collaboration Platform** > **Collaborations** tab > click the relevant collaboration to access it.
2. Click **Associate** under **Associate sources**.  

   <Image alt="Associate sources click associate.png" src="https://files.readme.io/ed192e758d04c3f48dd70877f90c39428f72460247d4a5940c1217393ae5f686-34014749769617.png" width="2080px" />
3. Select the sources you want to associate (or <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-create-and-manage-sources">create a new source</Anchor>).  

   <Image alt="select data view.png" src="https://files.readme.io/4f5df701745c0262d864407dd36cb26fe7649d63b9546ac9334d29bbe25296de-43774232835473.png" width="78.23%" />  
   Then click **Save**.
4. #### **Optional:** Set custom data permissions by source

   You can set custom permissions on a source (which differs from your general data permissions policy). **To set custom data permissions by source:**

   From the 3-dot menu next to the source, click **Edit permissions**.  

   <Image alt="Edit permissions new.png" src="https://files.readme.io/94395905bcd18545dfdaedb3829eafa37e047776ba6ba6d5daf0a2a00ca5afaa-43776633793553.png" width="1330px" />

   Set the permissions according to your preferences (<Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-create-and-manage-sources#grant-permissions-for-your-source">learn about permissions</Anchor>), then click **Save**.  

   <Image alt="Permissionsfor source1.png" src="https://files.readme.io/e4b025bf9cd944024e578ac4bcfd60c46d847b3e403b1da737e3451eb708e5a9-34019077412369.png" width="90.08%" />

   **Note:** You can also customize the source permissions of existing collaborations by going to **Associate sources**, within the collaboration and clicking the action (3 dot) menu to the right of the source > **Edit permissions**.

   4. Click **Create collaboration**.

</Accordion>

That's it! Your collaboration is ready. Now it's time for you and your collaborator to <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-build-and-activate-audiences">create audiences</Anchor> and run campaigns. Afterwards, <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-collaboration-measurement-overview">set up measurement</Anchor> and analyze the results to gain insights.

## Manage collaborations

This section describes how to manage your existing collaborations.

<div id="general-actions" />

<Accordion title="Associate or manage sources">

**To manage associated sources:**

1. In the Data Collaboration Platform (Data Clean Room), go to the **Collaborations** tab > specific collaboration.   

   <Image alt="From collaborations go to specific collaboration New.png" src="https://files.readme.io/b9c153e3e314f7788e942a49e465c9b10725066f50715a6eb0447f6796269328-43777319665169.png" width="876px" />
2. In the collaboration dashboard, click either the **Associated sources** section or the action menu on the upper right > **Associated sources**.  

   <Image alt="actions menu open.png" src="https://files.readme.io/6f77b81a05cdb6ac3f3084d0521ba084b8dae57f9ae7b8cdc823ef1cfa138636-34019992385553.png" width="1377px" />


#### Associate new sources

**To associate new sources:** Click **+ Associate source** > Select sources from the dropdown menu > Click **Save**.  

<Image alt="Associate source in aexisting collaboration.png" src="https://files.readme.io/4ecd2b8d33195d79c93848b8b1385f504b2c8ee78f509b99d7e4f3b1910bf81e-34127880603921.png" width="574px" />

#### Manage sources

**To manage sources already associated with the collaboration:** Click the action menu next to the source to:

* **Go to source** - This displays all the source details.
* **Edit permissions** - Customize the permission for that particular source (by default all sources share the permissions of the main **data permissions policy** you defined). <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-create-and-manage-sources#sharing-permissions">Learn more</Anchor>
* **Remove from collaboration** - This removes the source from your collaboration (the source will still appear in your platform under **Sources**).  
  **Note:** Removing a source from a collaboration means collaborators will no longer be able to view or use the source, associated <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/data-collaboration-platform-dcp-create-customized-data-views">data views</Anchor>, and associated audiences. The source will still be available in the platform under “Sources”.

</Accordion>

<div id="edit-or-suspend-a-collaboration" />

<Accordion title="Edit or suspend a collaboration">

**To edit, rename, or suspend a collaboration:**

1. In the Data Collaboration Platform (Data Clean Room), go to the **Collaborations** tab > specific collaboration.  

   <Image alt="From collaborations go to specific collaboration New.png" src="https://files.readme.io/b9c153e3e314f7788e942a49e465c9b10725066f50715a6eb0447f6796269328-43777319665169.png" width="876px" />
2. In the Collaboration dashboard, click the action (3 dots) menu at the top right and select the specific action.   

   <Image alt="actions menu open.png" src="https://files.readme.io/6f77b81a05cdb6ac3f3084d0521ba084b8dae57f9ae7b8cdc823ef1cfa138636-34019992385553.png" width="1377px" />

> 📘 **Notes on suspended collaborations**
>
> * Only a collaboration owner/creator can suspend a collaboration.
> * Suspending a collaboration suspends your collaborator's access to view your data and perform any actions with it. Audiences created from the collaboration and dynamic query will become unavailable.
> * Once a collaboration is suspended, you can reactivate it by clicking **Reactivate collaboration** from the same menu.

</Accordion>

<div id="invite-team-members" />

<Accordion title="Invite team members">

You can invite all or some members of your team to the collaboration. Once invited, they’ll be able to view sources and perform actions within the collaboration.

**To invite team members:**

1. In the Data Collaboration Platform (Data Clean Room), go to the **Collaborations** tab > specific collaboration.  

   <Image alt="From collaborations go to specific collaboration New.png" src="https://files.readme.io/b9c153e3e314f7788e942a49e465c9b10725066f50715a6eb0447f6796269328-43777319665169.png" width="876px" />
2. Click **Manage access** (on the upper right).   

   <Image alt="Manage access.png" src="https://files.readme.io/e9cfaf5da2b147f68255d6d442078a3770db4c9873109d1188b9a2b5c0f4e65f-36132545995281.png" width="475px" />
3. **To allow anyone at your company (with DCP permissions) to access the collaboration:**   

   Set the **General access** settings to **Anyone at "your company"**  

   <Image alt="Collaboration is draft - manage access dropdown w arrow.png" src="https://files.readme.io/6358d272f675d9f6f7ad4b07637db7361d772d34738bbd54488212c9870598b4-36132546008209.png" width="623px" />

   **To invite specific users:**   

   Set the **General access** settings to **Invited only**.  
   Enter names or emails in the search bar to search for users, then click **Invite users**  

   **<Image alt="search for users w arrow.png" src="https://files.readme.io/c32fc8962fbdda7c70dc218147c8f9bbc25e806e72bb00c18c7b50fbc3b373cb-36132546008721.png" width="622px" />**  

   Invited users will receive an email informing them of the invite. They will be able to view associated sources and perform actions within the collaboration.

</Accordion>

## Administer account

Enforce legal governance that applies to all collaborations.

<div id="terms-and-conditions" />

<Accordion title="Terms and conditions">

You can define and enforce a legal Terms and Conditions agreement that governs all your collaborations. Once published, all collaborators must explicitly accept your terms before accessing collaboration data.

#### Apply Terms and Conditions

**To apply Terms and Conditions:**

1. In the **Collaborations** tab, click the menu (3 dots) icon, on the upper right.

   <Image alt="Click Account settings.png" src="https://files.readme.io/c4fc038d615345fdca6552dc1fd3609298557c237da01512f9329c2f380d1c61-42204335554193.png" width="1776px" />
2. Click **Account settings**

   <Image alt="Apply Terms andConditions w arrow.png" src="https://files.readme.io/ec125bae5675efbfc20e16d726767c51894b5d3e4ec5d7d47073b0f14f6df90e-42204339679377.png" width="69.49%" />
3. From the **Terms and Conditions tab,** enter a valid URL in the **Terms and Conditions** field. This should be the URL of your official terms.
4. Click **Apply Terms and Conditions**. A confirmation message appears, indicating that all collaborators will be required to accept the updated Terms and Conditions.

   The new terms are now enforced for all your collaborations. Collaborators will need to confirm that they have read and accept your Terms and Conditions to collaborate with you. If a collaborator doesn't accept, they won't be able to access or perform actions in the collaboration.

#### Update existing Terms and Conditions

You can update the content (or URL) of your existing Terms and Conditions.

**To update :**

1. Make the desired changes to your official Terms and Conditions document.
2. From the menu (3 dots) icon, on the upper right, click **Account settings**.
3. If the URL has changed, enter the new URL in the **Terms and Conditions** field.
4. Click **Publish new terms**.  
   Collaborators will need to confirm that they have read and accept the new terms to continue collaborating.

> 📘 **Notes**
>
> * Terms and Conditions are mandatory for your collaborators; they cannot bypass or dismiss them.
> * The platform stores only a URL; it does not host or version the legal text.
> * Terms apply at the account level, governing all collaborations owned by the account.
> * If a collaborator doesn’t accept the Terms and Conditions while the collaboration is active, their non-acceptance won’t affect existing data or active audiences.

</Accordion>
