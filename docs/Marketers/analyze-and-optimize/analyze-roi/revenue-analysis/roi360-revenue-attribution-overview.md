> **At a glance:** Revenue data helps give you a clear and complete picture of ROI and ROAS. Learn about the different products and features available for advertisers to aggregate and view revenue data.

## Overview

Revenue is one-half of the ROI picture (the other half is cost). When you have accurate revenue data, attributed to the originating media sources and campaigns, you can:

* See the complete picture of your ROI and ROAS. Meaning, you can see which ad networks, campaigns, and ads bring the most valuable users, and not just installs or engagements.
* Adjust your ad spend and marketing campaigns accordingly.

### Premium revenue reporting

There are three main sources for generating revenue.

* **In-app purchases**: This includes revenue from the sale of any products or services inside your app. AppsFlyer is able to automatically validate and measure revenue from in-app purchases (including pending purchases, purchases, refunds, cancellations, taxes, app store commissions, etc.). For more information about in-app purchases, see <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/purchase-and-subscription-validation-overview">Purchase and subscription validation.</Anchor>
* **Subscription revenue**: revenue from subscriptions to your app or services. It also includes any changes to subscriptions, such as upgrades, cancellations, and refunds. For more information about Subscription revenue, see <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/purchase-and-subscription-validation-overview">Purchase and subscription validation.</Anchor>
* **Ad revenue**: This includes revenue generated by hosting ads and displaying them to your app users. For example, your app can display ads from other apps. If your users click these ads, you generate revenue per click. For more information about Ad revenue, see the <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/roi360-guide-ad-revenue-attribution">ROI360 guide - ad revenue attribution</Anchor>.

### Basic revenue reporting

AppsFlyer provides basic revenue measurement tools. These tools are less robust than the advanced revenue reporting methods and require a substantial amount of configuration by your developers, but they don't require a subscription to premium products.

* Revenue recording via <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/in-app-events-overview#revenue-in-in-app-events">customized in-app events</Anchor>. You can also share this data with ad networks via <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/in-app-event-postback-configuration#data-to-include-in-the-postbacks">postbacks</Anchor>. **Note**: Consider using validated in-app purchases (a premium product) to include the automatic measurement of refunds, taxes, app store commissions, etc.
* Receipt validation
