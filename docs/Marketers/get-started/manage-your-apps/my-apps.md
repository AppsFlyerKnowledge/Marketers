**At a glance:** View all your apps in AppsFlyer, search and filter them, pin your preferred apps, and perform specific actions for each app.

## Overview

![New-My-apps.jpg](https://files.readme.io/df286b15b469cb78093b3d2517f64f61246396bedf44932b5a344ac360242e19-small-27448775025937.jpg)

Explore the actions and functionalities available on the My Apps page:

### Add an app

[Add your app](/docs/adding-an-app-to-appsflyer) to AppsFlyer to get attribution data on installs and in-app events and analyze the data using the different tools on AppsFlyer.

- From the top-right of the **My Apps** page, click **+ Add app**.

### Add or view Brand bundles

[Brand bundles](/docs/pba-brand-bundles-and-settings) consolidate the attribution, events, visits, and revenue of brand-related apps and websites. Use the brand bundle settings page to mark conversion events, integrate with Google Ads, and more.

1. From the top-right of the **My Apps** page, click **Brand bundles**.
2. Select the relevant option:
   - **New brand bundle:** To [create a new brand bundle](/docs/pba-brand-bundles-and-settings#add-brand-bundle)
   - **View brand bundles**.: To [access your existing Brand bundles](/docs/pba-brand-bundles-and-settings#accessing-brand-bundles)

### Search and filter the list of apps

- **Search:** Use the search bar at the top-left to search by app name, ID, or brand bundle.
- **Filter:**Use the filters in the top-right menu to narrow the list by:
  - **Status:** The app status in the app store: **Available** or **Pending in store**. The status is updated every 4 hours. You can [refresh the status manually](/docs/my-apps#refresh-the-status-of-pending-apps).
  - **Platform:** Select platforms by which to filter the apps.

### Pin your preferred apps

Select up to 6 apps to pin to the top of the list.

- Hover over the left of the app logo and click the Pin ![pin.png](https://files.readme.io/2ae8d880fa15ce03067ce0532107c7d4c19c7c170a64296791a21a91edeb40fb-27439158632849.png) icon.

To unpin, click the Pin ![pin.png](https://files.readme.io/2ae8d880fa15ce03067ce0532107c7d4c19c7c170a64296791a21a91edeb40fb-27439158632849.png) icon next to the app.

### Switch between List and Card views

Select how to view your apps, by list (for many apps) or card (for fewer apps):

- From the top-right menu, click the List or View icon ![list-card-view.jpg](https://files.readme.io/b60e06a80cdf438bce0c9ec4db960ab60270060cc18328e78b1f9172e63608b1-27440175256081.jpg)

### Refresh the status of a pending app

The status of pending apps in the app store is updated every 4 hours. You can manually refresh the query of a specific app to see the updated status:

- Hover over the right of an app and click the Refresh icon ![refresh-app.jpg](https://files.readme.io/bc703db1f3cce8f2cefcb66dd07912226c99f91f7fb27d26600e14e42ef721ac-27440175257873.jpg).

### More options per app

Explore additional options available for the selected app:

- Hover over the right of a specific app row, click the Menu ![menu_icon.png](https://files.readme.io/2bf1c4b0bd3aed516f8e35722d4b1bac6e4fd45d9daaddd1ee7382918f9c0704-27448775026833.png) icon, and select from the following actions:

  - **App settings:** Directs you to the [settings page](/docs/app-settings) of the selected app.
  - **Go to store:**  Directs you to the app page in the app store.
  - **Transfer apps:** Enables you to select apps and [transfer them between AppsFlyer accounts](/docs/transferring-apps-between-accounts).
  - **Remove app:** Enables you to [remove an app](/docs/deleting-things-in-appsflyer#delete-an-app) from all views and dashboards with AppsFlyer stopping the recording of app-related data (after confirmation).

### Go to an app's Overview dashboard

- Click an app row or card to access the [Overview dashboard](/docs/overview-dashboard-user-acquisition-and-retargeting-ltv) of the app.
