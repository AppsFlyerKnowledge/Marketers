> **At a glance:** View all your apps in AppsFlyer, search and filter them, pin your preferred apps, and perform specific actions for each app.

## Overview

[![New-My-apps.jpg](https://files.readme.io/df286b15b469cb78093b3d2517f64f61246396bedf44932b5a344ac360242e19-small-27448775025937.jpg)](https://files.readme.io/df286b15b469cb78093b3d2517f64f61246396bedf44932b5a344ac360242e19-small-27448775025937.jpg)

Explore the actions and functionalities available on the My Apps page:

<div id="add-an-app" />

<Accordion title="Add an app">

<Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/adding-an-app-to-appsflyer">Add your app</Anchor> to AppsFlyer to get attribution data on installs and in-app events and analyze the data using the different tools on AppsFlyer.

* From the top-right of the **My Apps** page, click **+ Add app**.

</Accordion>

<div id="add-or-view-brand-bundles" />

<Accordion title="Add or view Brand bundles">

<Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/pba-brand-bundles-and-settings">Brand bundles</Anchor> consolidate the attribution, events, visits, and revenue of brand-related apps and websites. Use the brand bundle settings page to mark conversion events, integrate with Google Ads, and more.

1. From the top-right of the **My Apps** page, click **Brand bundles**.
2. Select the relevant option:
   * **New brand bundle:** To <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/pba-brand-bundles-and-settings#add-brand-bundle">create a new brand bundle</Anchor>
   * **View brand bundles**.: To <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/pba-brand-bundles-and-settings#accessing-brand-bundles">access your existing Brand bundles</Anchor>

</Accordion>

<div id="search-and-filter-the-list-of-apps" />

<Accordion title="Search and filter the list of apps">

* **Search:** Use the search bar at the top-left to search by app name, ID, or brand bundle.
* **Filter:** Use the filters in the top-right menu to narrow the list by:
  * **Status:** The app status in the app store: **Available** or **Pending in store**. The status is updated every 4 hours. You can [refresh the status manually](#refresh-the-status-of-pending-apps).
  * **Platform:** Select platforms by which to filter the apps.

</Accordion>

<div id="pin-your-preferred-apps" />

<Accordion title="Pin your preferred apps">

Select up to 6 apps to pin to the top of the list.

* Hover over the left of the app logo and click the Pin ![pin.png](https://files.readme.io/2ae8d880fa15ce03067ce0532107c7d4c19c7c170a64296791a21a91edeb40fb-27439158632849.png) icon.

To unpin, click the Pin ![pin.png](https://files.readme.io/2ae8d880fa15ce03067ce0532107c7d4c19c7c170a64296791a21a91edeb40fb-27439158632849.png) icon next to the app.

</Accordion>

<div id="switch-between-list-and-card-views" />

<Accordion title="Switch between List and Card views">

Select how to view your apps, by list (for many apps) or card (for fewer apps):

* From the top-right menu, click the List or View icon <Image alt="list-card-view.jpg" src="https://files.readme.io/b60e06a80cdf438bce0c9ec4db960ab60270060cc18328e78b1f9172e63608b1-27440175256081.jpg" width="47px" />

</Accordion>

<div id="refresh-the-status-of-pending-apps" />

<Accordion title="Refresh the status of a pending app">

The status of pending apps in the app store is updated every 4 hours. You can manually refresh the query of a specific app to see the updated status:

* Hover over the right of an app and click the Refresh icon <Image alt="refresh-app.jpg" src="https://files.readme.io/bc703db1f3cce8f2cefcb66dd07912226c99f91f7fb27d26600e14e42ef721ac-27440175257873.jpg" width="123px" />.

</Accordion>

<div id="more-app-options" />

<Accordion title="More options per app">

Explore additional options available for the selected app:

* Hover over the right of a specific app row, click the Menu ![menu_icon.png](https://files.readme.io/2bf1c4b0bd3aed516f8e35722d4b1bac6e4fd45d9daaddd1ee7382918f9c0704-27448775026833.png) icon, and select from the following actions:
  * **App settings:** Directs you to the <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/app-settings">settings page</Anchor> of the selected app.
  * **Go to store:**  Directs you to the app page in the app store.
  * **Transfer apps:** Enables you to select apps and <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/transferring-apps-between-accounts">transfer them between AppsFlyer accounts</Anchor>.
  * **Remove app:** Enables you to <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/deleting-things-in-appsflyer#remove-or-delete-an-app">remove an app</Anchor> from all views and dashboards with AppsFlyer stopping the recording of app-related data (after confirmation).

</Accordion>

<div id="go-to-an-apps-overview-dashboard" />

<Accordion title="Go to an app's Overview dashboard">

* Click an app row or card to access the Overview dashboard of the app.

</Accordion>
