Use Launchpad (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K) to quickly navigate to a page on the dashboard.

## Using Launchpad

**To open Launchpad:**

1. Enter as follows:
   - PC, click **Ctrl+K.**
   - Mac, click **Cmd+K.** The search bar displays.
2. Enter a search term.

The search results display.

Launchpad displays links to pages that you have permission to view.

![Launchpad_2.png](https://files.readme.io/618a539c5d0e8638d4789333b3053450bf7c4f00e384aa39ae8445d8133c9cdb-Launchpad_2.png)

3. Click the link in the search results to send you directly to the required result.
