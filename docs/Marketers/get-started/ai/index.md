Use AI to unlock insights, automate workflows, and drive smarter decisions.

<Cards>
  <Card title="[Beta] Agent Hub overview" href="/docs/beta-agent-hub-overview" icon="fa-solid fa-circle-info">
    Agent Hub is AppsFlyer's centralized platform for managing AI-driven agents that proactively deliver actionable marketing insights directly into your workflow. These agents constantly analyze your data to deliver valuable outputs and recommend actionable steps, all within your team's collaborative environment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Assistant" href="/docs/ai-assistant" icon="fa-solid fa-robot">
    The AI Assistant is your virtual analyst and platform knowledge helper in AppsFlyer. Use it to uncover insights, measure performance, identify top and low performers, explore campaign trends, and understand visual insights like tables and charts, either by asking questions directly in the AI Assistant or by using Analyze with AI from My Dashboards to investigate specific charts in context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="[Beta] AppsFlyer MCP" href="/docs/beta-appsflyer-mcp" icon="fa-solid fa-file-lines">
    AppsFlyer MCP connects your AppsFlyer data to large language models (LLMs), such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini CLI, Copilot CLI, and VS Code. Ask questions, get performance insights, and retrieve AppsFlyer information directly in your AI chat or development environment.
  </Card>
</Cards>
