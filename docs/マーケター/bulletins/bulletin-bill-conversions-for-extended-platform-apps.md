| **What's new** | Starting February 1, 2026, non-organic installs from extended platform apps (for example, CTV, PC, and console) are included in billable conversions, according to your existing conversion-based commercial terms. |
| --- | --- |
| **Effective date** | February 1, 2026 |
| **Who's affected** | Customers with extended platform apps on conversion packages or Adaptive Pricing. |
| **Recommended action** | Review expected conversion volume and Adaptive Pricing credit usage. |
| **Additional information** | Billing applies to conversions generated by extended platform apps. This includes apps running on CTV, PC, console, and other non-mobile platforms that aren’t iOS, Android, Windows phone, PBA, or website.<br /><br />**<span id="what-you-should-know"></span>What you should know**<ul><li>Only **non-organic installs** are billed</li><li>**Retargeting isn’t supported** for extended platforms</li><li>**Owned media activity** is included</li><li>Conversion unit cost is **the same as mobile conversions**</li><li>No additional fees apply at launch</li></ul>**<span id="adaptive-pricing-considerations"></span>Adaptive Pricing considerations**<br /><br />If you use Adaptive Pricing, billing extended platform conversions can increase credit consumption related to:<ul><li>Non-organic installs</li><li>ROI360 cost and revenue measurement</li><li>Pull API rows</li><li>Data Locker raw and aggregated reports</li></ul>**What’s not included**<ul><li>MAU-based billing isn’t currently supported for extended platform apps</li></ul> |
