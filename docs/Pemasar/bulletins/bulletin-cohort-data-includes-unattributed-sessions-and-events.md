| **What's new** | <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/id/docs/measure-reinstalls#in-app-event-dan-bagaimana-mereka-dikaitkan">Unattributed sessions and events</Anchor> appear in the Cohort dashboard and reports. |
| --- | --- |
| **Effective date** | May 26, 2024 |
| **Where** | <ul><li>Cohort and Retention dashboard</li><li>Cohort API</li><li>Cohort reports via Data Locker</li><li>Advanced aggregated reports via Data Locker [Closed beta]</li></ul> |
| **What you must know** | <ul><li>Until the effective date, <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/id/docs/measure-reinstalls#in-app-event-dan-bagaimana-mereka-dikaitkan">unattributed sessions and events</Anchor> weren't included in the Cohort dashboard and reports. This sometimes caused discrepancies between Cohort other reporting methods, where Cohort showed fewer organic sessions and events (and revenue).</li><li>From the effective date, unattributed sessions and events appear in the Cohort dashboard and reports with "organic" media source. This means:<ul><li>You may see more organic data in Cohort data than before.</li><li>There won't be any more discrepancies between Cohort and other reporting methods caused by unattributed sessions and events.</li></ul></li></ul> |
