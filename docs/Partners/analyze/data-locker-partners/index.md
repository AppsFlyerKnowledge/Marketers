Receive advertiser attribution data in your cloud storage

<Cards>
  <Card title="Single Source of Truth (SSOT) Data Locker report for ad networks" href="/docs/single-source-of-truth-ssot-data-locker-report-for-ad-networks" icon="fa-solid fa-handshake">
    The Single Source of Truth (SSOT) report in Data Locker offers a unified and accurate overview of app campaign performances by merging data from various attribution methods, such as ID matching and SKAN. It provides a way to load data into your BI systems to get the highest accuracy of data.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Locker reports for API partners" href="/docs/data-locker-reports-for-api-partners" icon="fa-solid fa-handshake">
    Reports listed in this article are available to you (API partners) in your Data Locker. Before we can share the data with you, the advertiser (app owner) must grant you data access as detailed in this article.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Locker for partners" href="/docs/data-locker-for-partners" icon="fa-solid fa-handshake">
    Data Locker for partners (ad networks and agencies) delivers app data to the partner's storage in AWS, GCS, or BigQuery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Locker—reports available for ad networks" href="/docs/data-locker-reports-available-for-ad-networks" icon="fa-solid fa-handshake">
    Reports listed are available to ad networks via Data Locker. Advertisers must give the ad network explicit permission.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Locker reports for agencies" href="/docs/data-locker-reports-for-agencies" icon="fa-solid fa-box-archive">
    Reports listed in this article are available to you (agencies) in your Data Locker. Before we can share the data with you, the advertiser (app owner) must grant you data access as detailed in this article.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ad revenue signals for UA ad networks" href="/docs/ad-revenue-signals-for-ua-ad-networks" icon="fa-solid fa-handshake">
    Ad networks responsible for user acquisition can receive data regarding ad revenue and use it to optimize their customers' UA campaigns.
  </Card>
</Cards>
