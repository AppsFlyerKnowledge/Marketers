At a glance: Reports listed are available to ad networks via Data Locker. Advertisers must give the ad network explicit permission.

## Reports available for ad networks

| Report type (topic) | Which data access permissions are required? | Data freshness\* | Available to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Aggregated campaign performance](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/360020834237) | - Aggregated conversions<br>- Aggregated in-app events<br>- Aggregated revenue | Daily | Ad networks |
| [Ad revenue signals for UA ad networks](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/4402898251409) | - Access ad revenue raw data | Daily | Ad networks |
| [Blocked clicks & impressions](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/4420246395793) | - No advertiser permissions required | Daily | Ad networks |
| [Failed click signatures](https://support.appsflyer.com/hc/articles/4420246395793) | - No advertiser permissions required | Daily | Ad networks |
| [Single Source of Truth (SSOT)](/docs/single-source-of-truth-ssot-data-locker-report-for-ad-networks) | - Aggregated conversions<br>- Aggregated in-app events<br>- Aggregated revenue | Daily | Ad networks |
| **\* Key to abbreviations**<br>**Daily**:<br>- Reports are streamed to the h=23 folder.<br>- These reports are typically available by 07:00-12:00 UTC in the h=23 folder of the preceding day.<br>- For example, the report for data generated on Monday is in the Monday h=23 folder.<br>- The data is available on Tuesday:  - For aggregated campaign performance: After 10:00 UTC.<br>  - For ad revenue signals: After 21:00 UTC.<br>  - For blocked clicks & impressions, and failed click signatures: After 07:00 UTC. |
