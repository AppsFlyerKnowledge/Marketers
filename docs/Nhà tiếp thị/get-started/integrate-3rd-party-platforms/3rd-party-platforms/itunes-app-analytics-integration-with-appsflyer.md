> **At a glance:** View attribution data in iTunes Connect by integrating iTunes App Analytics with AppsFlyer.

<div id="enabling-the-integration" />

## Enabling the integration

<Image align="center" alt="itunes-connect-app-analytics.png" src="https://files.readme.io/e1e480c1af63a3404e40ba4049dac13497a4db8b298a1735812524392232f2d9-11523192862353.png" />

To enable the integration simply send the iTunes Provider ID to your AppsFlyer Account Manager:

<Image align="center" alt="itunes-connect-app-analytics2.png" src="https://files.readme.io/7ae2c205b45a9084752a85c4bec93cb052f0e288a1dbee97c1eb5e51cd66d730-11523234132753.png" />

**Known Limitation:** The integration only works with networks that redirect their clicks through AppsFlyer. SRNs (for example, Meta ads, X Ads, Google Ads, etc.) are currently not supported.
